MyAgent

MyAgent is a personal AI email assistant used by its developer for their own email. It is not a public service: there are no user accounts, no sign-up and no login, and it only connects to the developer's own Google account.

Purpose of MyAgent

  • Prepares email drafts, which are sent only after the owner approves each individual message.
  • Detects replies to those emails, so follow-up reminders stop as soon as someone answers.
  • Recognises delivery failures and unsubscribe requests, so they are respected.

How MyAgent uses Google data

MyAgent uses the Gmail API with two permissions, only on the owner's own account:

  • gmail.readonly: to read replies, delivery failures and unsubscribe requests.
  • gmail.send: to send emails the owner has individually reviewed and approved.

MyAgent does not delete or modify emails and does not access contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google service. All data stays on the owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models. MyAgent's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details are in the MyAgent Privacy Policy.

Acceptable use

MyAgent only processes text emails. It does not generate, edit or store images of people, and it is never used to create sexual, intimate or non-consensual content of any kind. MyAgent complies with the Google APIs Terms of Service.

MyAgent – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

About MyAgent

MyAgent is a personal AI email assistant used only by its developer for their own email. It is not offered to other users. The only Google account it accesses is the developer's own

Data collected

  • Email messages and metadata (sender, recipient, subject, date, thread) in the owner's own Gmail account, used only to detect replies, delivery failures and unsubscribe requests.
  • Emails that the owner approves to send, and a record that they were sent.

No data is collected from any other person's Google account, and no data is used for advertising, profiling, or creating images of people.

Google permissions used

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly: read-only access, to recognise replies, delivery failures and unsubscribe requests.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send: to send emails the owner has reviewed and explicitly approved, one message at a time.

MyAgent does not delete or modify emails and does not access contacts, calendar, Dri other Google service.

How data is used

  • Gmail data is used only to provide MyAgent's features to its owner.
  • No email is sent without the owner's explicit approval of that exact message.
  • Gmail data is never sold, rented, shared for advertising, or used to train AI mod
  • Email content is analysed by AI models running locally on the owner's computer.No person other than the owner reads the data.

Data sharing

Google user data is not shared with, transferred to, or sold to any third party, exc AI provider when the owner chooses to use one to draft a message, limited to the minimu needed, with no use for advertising or model training.

Storage and security

  • All data is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private local database.
  • Google authorization tokens are stored encrypted on that computer.
  • MyAgent has no public servers and does not upload Gmail data to any website.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MyAgent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while MyAgent is in use and can be deleted at any time by deleting database. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions after which MyAgent can no longer read or send email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
